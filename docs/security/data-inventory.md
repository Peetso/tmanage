# Data inventory (seed)

Plan item 53-20 (PRIV-53-52), seeded from `apps/api/src/common/database/schema.ts` `TABLES`. 56-18 replaces this seed with the fuller inventory; the test contract stays.

## How to read it

- **Personal fields**: columns that name, contact, track or quote a person, found from the column names. A dash means none.
- **Erasure rule**: what must happen to the personal data when a person or company is erased.
  - `delete`: the row goes.
  - `pseudonymise`: the row stays, without anything that identifies the person.
  - `keep-fiscal-redacted`: kept for a legal reason (audit trail, fiscal record), with personal fields redacted.
  - `none-no-personal-data`: nothing personal to erase.
- **Today**: `enforced` means the erasure job (or the named age-out purge) does it now; `retained` means the row is kept on purpose and the notes give the legal or integrity reason; `gap` means the rule is not carried out yet and the owner item fixes it; `n/a` means there is nothing to do. `data-inventory.doc.test.ts` holds the totals below to the rows.
- **Owner**: the plan item that owns the rule. H3 is the company erasure (`apps/api/src/modules/privacy/privacy-erasure.repository.ts`), run by the privacy job (`privacy-job.ts`).

## What erasure does today

- **Account deletion** (`/me/privacy/deletion`): after the grace period the privacy job offboards the membership. The person can no longer sign in to that company, and their personal access tokens stop working. A day later `AccountErasure` (56-18) nulls their IP and user agent on that company's invoice event log and, once they belong to no organization at all, deletes their credentials, sessions and linked sign-ins, strips IP and user agent from their sign-in log, and pseudonymises the account row (shared work stays, shown as a former member).
- **Company deletion** (`/admin/privacy/deletion`): the job closes the company, and one day later empties every tenant table except the seven in `RETAINED_TABLES` (organizations, audit_events, audit_heads, audit_anchors, audit_retention_policy, privacy_exports, privacy_deletion_requests), plus the OAuth codes and tokens issued under its grants.
- **Audit-log retention** (56-18, 0358): for a workspace that set a window, audit entries older than it are purged in batches and the purge is itself audited; a workspace that never set one keeps its trail.
