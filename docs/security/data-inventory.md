# Data inventory (seed)

Plan item 53-20 (PRIV-53-52), seeded from `apps/api/src/common/database/schema.ts` `TABLES`. 56-18 replaces this seed with the fuller inventory; the test contract stays.

## How to read it

- **Personal fields**: columns that name, contact, track or quote a person, found from the column names. A dash means none.
- **Erasure rule**: what must happen to the personal data when a person or company is erased.
  - `delete`: the row goes.
  - `pseudonymise`: the row stays, without anything that identifies the person.
  - `keep-fiscal-redacted`: kept for a legal reason (audit trail, fiscal record), with personal fields redacted.
  - `none-no-personal-data`: nothing personal to erase.
- **Today**: `enforced` means the erasure job does it now and `priv-053-erasure-inventory.db.test.ts` proves it; `gap` means the rule is not carried out yet and the owner item fixes it; `n/a` means there is nothing to do.
- **Owner**: the plan item that owns the rule. H3 is the company erasure (`apps/api/src/modules/privacy/privacy-erasure.repository.ts`), run by the privacy job (`privacy-job.ts`).

## What erasure does today

- **Account deletion** (`/me/privacy/deletion`): after the grace period the privacy job offboards the membership. The person can no longer sign in to that company, and their personal access tokens stop working. Their rows and the global account stay.
- **Company deletion** (`/admin/privacy/deletion`): the job closes the company, and one day later empties every tenant table except the seven in `RETAINED_TABLES` (organizations, audit_events, audit_heads, audit_anchors, audit_retention_policy, privacy_exports, privacy_deletion_requests), plus the OAuth codes and tokens issued under its grants.
- Nothing pseudonymises or redacts yet. Those rows are marked `gap`.
