## Client ceiling review

`packages/domain/src/policies/client-ceiling.ts` holds `CLIENT_CAPABILITY_ALLOWLIST`; `client-ceiling.test.ts` proves the four client roles stay inside it. `requireClientCeiling` (`apps/api/src/modules/tenancy/role-grant-ceiling.ts`) refuses a client role carrying an internal capability with 422 `client_ceiling`, and the policy simulator returns a `client_internal_capability` warning for one. The guard is exported for the custom-role and member-role write paths; wiring it into those paths is their owners' change.
