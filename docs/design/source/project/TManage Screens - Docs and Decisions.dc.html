DECISION RECORD · ATLAS WEBSITE RELAUNCH
Homepage direction
Maya Hassan · updated 26 minutes ago · 3 collaborators
The homepage has to do one job before anything else: get a guest into the booking flow. Everything in this direction follows from that, and the two alternatives we rejected failed on the same point.
The decision
The booking call to action stays above the fold on every breakpoint, and the offers strip moves below the rooms grid. Arabic and English layouts carry the same hierarchy.
▤
Homepage Concept v2
DELIVERABLE · IN CLIENT REVIEW · YOUSSEF KAMAL · 26H
live object
Why not the alternatives
A full-width offers hero tested well on desktop and pushed booking below the fold on phones, which is where most guests are. A search-first header solved discovery for returning guests only, and Atlas told us in discovery that most traffic is new.
