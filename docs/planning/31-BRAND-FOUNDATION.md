# TManage — Brand Foundation and Product Content Direction

> **Visual-direction supersession (14 August 2026):** Brand idea, voice, terminology, and messaging remain valid. The older restrained blue/indigo visual paragraph is replaced by Limeframe in files 39–40 and the primary visual reference analysis in file 47.

## 1. Brand status

`TManage` is the selected working/product name. Commercial use remains conditional on professional trademark/domain clearance described in `32-NAME-DOMAIN-CLEARANCE.md`. Designers use the name but avoid final logo/domain lockup until the gate passes.

## 2. Brand idea

**Connected work, clearly delivered.** TManage turns conversations, commitments, execution, client actions, and approval into one accountable story.

## 3. Positioning

For agencies, studios, consultancies, and software service teams managing internal delivery and client outcomes, TManage is the work operating system that connects communication, project execution, knowledge, deliverables, and approval. Unlike separate chat/task/doc tools or generic guest access, TManage gives teams full internal context and clients a safe action-focused portal built from the same work graph.

## 4. Promise and proof pillars

- **Nothing gets lost:** message/request becomes linked work with source context.
- **Clients know what to do:** action queue, milestones, dates, and one governed version.
- **Approval means approval:** actor, authority, timestamp, criteria, version/hash, history.
- **Private by default:** clear audience, real client preview, tenant isolation.
- **Less reporting work:** explainable pulse derived from current work, published by a human.

## 5. Audience-specific value

- Owner: see delivery risk without micromanaging people.
- Manager: replace chasing and manual status translation.
- Member: know what matters next with complete context and fewer interruptions.
- Client: understand progress and complete reviews without learning the team’s PM system.
- Admin: govern access, external collaboration, audit, and offboarding confidently.

## 6. Voice

Calm, direct, capable, respectful. Use concrete verbs and consequences. Prefer “Publish to 2 client reviewers” over “Submit.” Prefer “Due 2 days ago” over blame. Explain system reasoning and what happens next. Never celebrate overload, shame delay, or imply employee surveillance.

English voice: short sentence, plain business language, internationally understandable. Arabic voice: Modern Standard Arabic with clear product language; avoid stiff literal translation and unneeded English except established terms during initial bilingual market validation.

## 7. Naming system

Product: TManage. Major surfaces: TManage Home, Inbox, Chat, Work, Docs, Insights, Client Portal. Objects use plain singular nouns: Project, Task, Request, Deliverable, Approval, Decision, Update. Avoid branded names for ordinary features until value is proven.

## 8. Messaging hierarchy

Primary headline: **All the work. One connected story.**

Supporting line: **Plan, communicate, deliver, and get client approval without rebuilding context across separate tools.**

Client message: **Know what’s moving, what’s due, and what needs your approval.**

Internal message: **Turn every commitment into visible, accountable work.**

Security message: **Internal by default. Client-ready when you choose.**

These are baseline content directions, subject to final design/market-language testing.

## 9. Visual direction — Limeframe

Personality: bold editorial clarity, intelligent optimism, high trust, and serious operational usefulness. The owner-supplied primary reference establishes a near-black/warm-paper/concrete/acid-lime language with geometric typography, capsules, circular controls, large-radius surfaces, disciplined asymmetry, whitespace, and controlled dark-panel overlap.

Lime means active/current/forward; success, warning, danger, and client boundary retain separate semantic colors. The expressive language is strongest on overview and client surfaces and becomes denser—without becoming generic—on lists, chat, docs, audit, and administration. Files 39–40 and 47 are the exact authority. Avoid gradients, excessive glass, card soup, childish gamification, fake productivity graphs, and competitor imitation.

## 10. Brand content checklist

Logo/wordmark after legal gate; favicon/app icon; light/dark; social card; landing copy; sign-in/invitation email; onboarding; empty/error/permission states; notification templates; security/trust center; English/Arabic terminology glossary; email subject/preheader/body/action; status/incident voice.

## 11. Terminology glossary

- Committed date: formally agreed current delivery date.
- Forecast date: current evidence-based prediction; may differ from committed.
- Desired date: client-requested date not yet committed.
- Client visible: published related clients may see it.
- Client action required: named client users must act.
- Governed version: exact immutable version under review/approval.
- Internal note: team-only object; never part of client comment.

Design and engineering must use these terms consistently.
