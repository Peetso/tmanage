# TManage — Research Evidence, Competitive Patterns, and Validation

## 1. Research status

This planning pass uses reputable public research, primary product/technical documentation, and practitioner discussions organized by role. Vendor research and online communities have commercial/self-selection bias; therefore claims are directional. With interviews unavailable, desk research is the discovery baseline, while prototype evidence, telemetry, support signals, and paid-alpha behavior become the validation path.

## 2. Evidence summary

- Microsoft Work Trend Index 2023: survey of 31,000 people in 31 countries; 64% struggled with time/energy, 68% lacked uninterrupted focus, 62% spent too much time seeking information, and Microsoft 365 activity averaged 57% communication vs 43% creation.
- Microsoft’s later work research continues to identify email and meeting load, and reports measurable time reductions from AI-assisted summarization in selected company studies; these support summarization as an aid, not proof of universal ROI.
- Asana’s Anatomy of Work hub reports global knowledge-worker research and frames “work about work,” process friction, and unnecessary meetings as material problems.
- Slack’s work operating system framing explicitly describes context fragmented across email, conversations, apps, and dashboards. It is competitor positioning, but aligns with the independent Microsoft signals.
- Asana guest documentation demonstrates the common market pattern: guests see explicitly shared objects. TManage differentiates by extending this into a client-specific action and delivery experience, while keeping the safe explicit-sharing principle.

## 3. Competitive pattern map

| Category | Strength | Common gap/opportunity for TManage |
|---|---|---|
| Slack/Teams | Fast communication and ecosystem | Commitments, decisions, files, and delivery context fragment |
| Asana/Linear/Jira | Structured execution and workflows | Communication and client experience often secondary |
| Notion/Confluence | Flexible docs and knowledge | Accountability/state can become inconsistent |
| ClickUp/Monday | Broad configurable suite | Cognitive/configuration load and uneven coherence risk |
| Basecamp | Simplicity and client collaboration philosophy | Less depth for complex work graphs, reporting, and automation |
| Google/Microsoft suites | Ubiquity and creation/meeting tools | Work state spans multiple apps and permission models |

The goal is not feature parity. Benchmark only the target loop: request, context, commitment, execution, review, approval, and delivery.

## 4. Key hypotheses to test

1. Service teams will consolidate new client projects if TManage reduces status and approval friction without forcing historical migration.
2. A dedicated client portal improves client participation compared with generic guest access.
3. Message-to-work traceability reduces missed commitments and duplicate entry.
4. Explainable project pulse reduces recurring status meetings.
5. Opinionated workflows outperform full customization for activation.
6. Clients value free/simple reviewer access enough to influence team purchase.
7. Teams accept one product only if search, notifications, and permissions are trustworthy.

## 5. Interview guide

Ask for a recent specific project, not preferences. Reconstruct: tools used; where request began; when it became a commitment; how owner/deadline were set; where files lived; how decisions were recorded; how status was communicated; latest delay or error; client review; permission concern; manual workaround; cost/consequence.

Avoid asking “Would you use TManage?” Show a prototype only after current behavior is understood. Ask for commitment: design-partner time, pilot data, real project, or willingness to pay.

## 6. Usability research scorecard

Record task success, time, errors, assistance, confidence, audience comprehension, and qualitative friction. Critical tests: accidental external sharing; selecting correct artifact version; client understanding of progress; task conversion scope; offboarding; approval invalidation after change.

## 8. Evidence discipline

- Re-check market, pricing, product, and technical claims at the implementation decision date.
- Preserve source date, sample size, geography, and sponsor in the research repository.
- Separate observed behavior, participant quote, interpretation, and product decision.
- Do not turn a vendor statistic into a guaranteed business case.
