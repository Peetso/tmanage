# TManage — Preliminary Name and Domain Clearance

## 1. Result

**Status: Yellow / not cleared for commercial launch.** Continue using TManage throughout product planning and private internal prototypes, but do not invest in a final logo, public launch, paid acquisition, company naming, or trademark filing until a qualified trademark attorney/search provider clears target jurisdictions and classes.

## 2. Findings as of 13 August 2026

- `tmanage.com` is registered. RDAP information indexed in June 2026 reports registration on 7 April 2026, expiry 7 April 2027, and Afternic nameservers, suggesting the exact `.com` is unavailable or offered for resale.
- Historical US `TMANAGE` registrations/applications existed for telecommuting, network, business, and software-related services. One reported registration (serial 76004489) was cancelled in 2008; cancellation does not prove the name is safe now or in other jurisdictions/classes.
- Public records and company databases continue to reference a historical TManage, Inc. and earlier technology/network services.
- Search results alone cannot establish current worldwide trademark rights, common-law use, likelihood of confusion, corporate name availability, or app-store conflicts.

## 3. Risk interpretation

The mark is suggestive/descriptive (“team/manage” or “T/manage”), making distinctiveness and search noise concerns likely. Historical adjacent technology use and the exact `.com` registration increase brand/domain cost and confusion risk. This does not mean use is prohibited; it means professional clearance is necessary.

## 4. Required legal search scope

- Exact and confusingly similar word/phonetic/spacing variants: TManage, T Manage, T-Manage, TeamManage, TManager, T Management.
- Target classes likely including Nice 9 (downloadable software), 42 (SaaS/software services), and relevant 35/38/41 depending final offering.
- Jurisdictions: country of incorporation, Egypt/MENA launch markets, US, UK, EU, and other planned sales territories.
- Official databases: Egyptian Trademark Office through counsel, WIPO Global Brand Database, USPTO, EUIPO/TMview, UK IPO, relevant national registries.
- Common-law/web/company/app-store/social-handle/domain search.
- Legal opinion on likelihood of confusion and filing strategy.

## 5. Domain decision gate

Do not attempt acquisition through personal contact without a budget/privacy strategy. Options after legal clearance:

1. Acquire `tmanage.com` through a reputable escrow/broker within approved ceiling.
2. Use a qualified alternate such as `gettmanage.com`, `tmanagehq.com`, or regional domain—but verify live availability and trademark implications at purchase time.
3. Rename before public launch if clearance/acquisition cost is disproportionate.

All planning contracts use `.example` placeholders until the domain decision.
