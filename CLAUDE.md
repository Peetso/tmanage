# TManage — read first

**The system design and the backend architecture are the most important thing in this project** (Peter, 2026-11-20).

Before you design, build, review or deploy anything here, read `docs/architecture/README.md` (read order; the edit gate asks for it once per session), and follow the general standards it points to. Where a part has a `PART.md`, read that too before editing the part; most parts have none, and that is intended (Peter, 2026-11-20: general standards, not a doc for every part). The standards: ENGINEERING-STANDARD, IMPLEMENTATION-PATTERNS, UI-UX-DESIGN-STANDARD, DESIGN-CHECKLIST. Quote the rule ids you apply. If a task cannot meet a rule, stop and fix the design. Never take a shortcut.
